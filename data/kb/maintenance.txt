MAINTENANCE INTERVALS AND FLUIDS:
Every 10 Hours or Daily: Walk around inspection. Check engine oil level. Check coolant level. Check hydraulic oil level. Inspect track tension and wear. Check for fluid leaks. Clean debris from engine compartment.
Every 50 Hours: Grease the boom, stick, and bucket linkages (12 grease points). Check tire pressure or track tension. Inspect seat belt and all safety devices. Clean air filter pre-cleaner.
Every 250 Hours: Change engine oil and replace engine oil filter. Inspect and clean battery terminals. Check alternator belt tension. Inspect fuel system for leaks.
Every 500 Hours: Replace primary and secondary fuel filters. Replace hydraulic tank breather filter. Inspect track rollers and idlers. Check and adjust bucket linkage wear.
Every 1000 Hours: Change hydraulic oil return filter. Change final drive oil. Inspect hydraulic cylinders for seal leaks. Replace air filter primary element.
Every 2000 Hours: Complete hydraulic oil change. Replace all fuel and hydraulic filters simultaneously. Inspect swing bearing and swing gear backlash.
Every 3000 Hours: Replace final drive gear oil. Full hydraulic system flush. Inspect engine mounts.

RECOMMENDED FLUIDS:
Engine Oil: CAT DEO 15W-40 or equivalent API CI-4 or CJ-4.
Hydraulic Fluid: CAT HYDO Advanced 10 (ISO VG 46).
Coolant: CAT ELC Extended Life Coolant, pre-mixed 50/50.
Final Drive Oil: CAT FDAO SAE 80W-90.
Grease: CAT Multipurpose Grease (NLGI Grade 2).
DEF (AdBlue): 32.5% urea solution, ISO 22241 standard.

WARNING SIGNS THAT REQUIRE IMMEDIATE SERVICE:
Hydraulic oil temperature above 90 degrees Celsius — stop and allow cooling.
Unusual hydraulic noise such as whining or knocking — inspect for cavitation.
Engine oil pressure warning light on — stop engine immediately.
Coolant temperature above 100 degrees Celsius — stop and check coolant level.
Track coming off rollers — stop and re-tension track.
Bucket curl or arm movement slower than normal — check hydraulic pressure.
Black smoke from exhaust — check air filter and fuel injectors.
Blue smoke from exhaust — engine burning oil, check oil level and seals.
White smoke on startup in warm weather — possible coolant leak into combustion.

MAINTENANCE LOG BEST PRACTICES:
Always record maintenance date, hours, and technician name.
Keep service records to avoid voiding warranty.
Use only genuine CAT parts for critical components.
Pre-delivery inspection must be completed within first 50 hours.
