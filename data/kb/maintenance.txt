MAINTENANCE INTERVALS AND FLUIDS:
Every 50 Hours: Grease the boom, stick, and bucket linkages. Check tire pressure or track tension.
Every 250 Hours: Change engine oil and replace engine oil filter.
Every 500 Hours: Replace primary and secondary fuel filters. Replace hydraulic tank breather filter.
Every 1000 Hours: Change hydraulic oil return filter. Change final drive oil.
Recommended Engine Oil: CAT DEO 15W-40.
Recommended Hydraulic Fluid: CAT HYDO Advanced 10 (ISO VG 46).
