EXCAVATOR 320 SPECIFICATIONS:
Operating Weight: 22,500 kg (49,600 lbs).
Engine Model: CAT C4.4 ACERT. Net Power: 164 HP (122 kW).
Maximum Digging Depth: 6.7 meters (22 feet).
Maximum Reach at Ground Level: 9.8 meters (32 feet).
Fuel Tank Capacity: 345 Liters (91 Gallons).
DEF Tank Capacity: 33 Liters (8.7 Gallons).
Track Width: 790 mm (31 inches) for standard swamp pads.
Bucket Capacity: Standard 1.19 m3. Available 0.57–1.9 m3.
Maximum Travel Speed: 5.5 km/h (3.4 mph) in high gear.
Ground Pressure: 49 kPa (7.1 psi) with standard shoes.
Swing Speed: 11.2 rpm.
Maximum Drawbar Pull: 201 kN (45,200 lbf).
Hydraulic System Pressure: 34,300 kPa (4,975 psi) implement circuit.
Cooling System Capacity: 26 Liters.
Engine Oil Capacity: 22 Liters with filter.
Hydraulic Tank Capacity: 215 Liters.

SAFE OPERATING LIMITS:
Maximum safe tilt angle: 15 degrees on any slope.
Maximum operating speed on grade: 5 km/h on slopes greater than 15 degrees.
Minimum operator weight: 50 kg. Maximum: 130 kg.
Always fasten seatbelt before starting engine.
Never swing the load over the cab area.
Never operate with hydraulic pressure above 320 bar continuously.
Engine temperature should stay below 100 degrees Celsius during normal operation.
RPM should not exceed 2100 RPM during normal work cycles.
Idle time above 30 minutes wastes fuel and causes engine carbon buildup.
Workers must stay at least 5 meters away from the machine during operation.

CAT 320 ATTACHMENTS:
Standard bucket: 1.2 m3 capacity for general earth excavation.
Heavy duty bucket: For rock and demolition work.
Hydraulic thumb: For gripping irregular objects.
Auger attachment: For drilling holes up to 1.2 m diameter.
Compactor plate: For trench backfill compaction.
Quick coupler: Allows attachment change in under 30 seconds.
Breaker/hammer: For concrete demolition, max 1,500 kg class.
Tiltrotator: Allows 360 degree bucket rotation with 40 degree tilt.
