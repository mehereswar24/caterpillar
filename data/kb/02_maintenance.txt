CAT 320 EXCAVATOR — COMPLETE MAINTENANCE MANUAL

DAILY / PRE-SHIFT CHECKS (EVERY 8-10 HOURS)
Walk around the machine before every shift. Check for fluid leaks under the machine — oil, fuel, hydraulic fluid, coolant. Inspect all hoses, lines, and fittings for cracks, wear, or loose connections. Check the engine oil level using the dipstick — add CAT DEO 15W-40 if below the low mark. Check engine coolant level in the overflow bottle — should be between MIN and MAX marks. Check hydraulic oil level in the sight glass on the hydraulic tank. Inspect track tension and track shoe condition on both sides. Check for missing or broken track pins or shoes. Test all lights, the horn, the reverse travel alarm, and the windshield wipers before moving. Inspect the seatbelt for fraying, cuts, or latch damage — replace immediately if damaged. Check all mirrors and camera lenses for dirt or damage. Verify all cab windows are clean and unobstructed. Log any defects found before starting work.

EVERY 50 HOURS
Lubricate all greasing points: boom foot pin (2 points), boom cylinder rod pin (1 point), boom cylinder base pin (1 point), stick cylinder rod pin (1 point), stick cylinder base pin (1 point), stick to boom pin (1 point), bucket cylinder rod pin (1 point), bucket cylinder base pin (1 point), bucket linkage pins (2 points), bucket pin (1 point), and swing bearing (4 points). Use CAT Multipurpose Grease (NLGI Grade 2). Check and adjust track tension — correct sag measured at midpoint of upper track run is 10-15 mm for standard conditions, 20-30 mm for muddy/clay conditions. Inspect and clean the air cleaner dust unloader valve. Check the engine air filter restriction indicator — replace primary element if indicator is red. Inspect fuel water separator and drain water if present.

EVERY 250 HOURS
Change engine oil and engine oil filter. Use CAT DEO 15W-40 (SAE 15W-40 API CJ-4 or higher). Drain hot oil for complete flushing. Replace with 22 Litres fresh oil plus new filter. Inspect alternator belt for tension and wear — deflection should be 10-12 mm at midpoint. Check battery terminals for corrosion — clean with baking soda solution if corroded. Inspect all electrical cables and connectors for chafing or loose connections. Check swing gear oil level — add CAT HYDO Advanced 10 if below sight glass. Inspect boom, stick, and cylinder pins for wear — replace if clearance exceeds 2 mm. Check fuel tank cap and breather for obstruction.

EVERY 500 HOURS
Replace primary and secondary fuel filters. Replace hydraulic tank breather filter. Replace engine air filter primary element (earlier if indicator triggers). Inspect and measure track roller, carrier roller, and idler wear — replace if wear exceeds service limits in Shop Manual. Check swing bearing backlash — maximum allowable is 3 mm at outer edge. Inspect all hydraulic cylinders for seal leaks — wipe clean and check after 8 hours operation. Inspect boom, stick, and bucket for cracks, especially around welds — do not operate if cracks found. Check hydraulic pump drive coupling for wear. Change final drive oil (4.0 L each side, CAT FDAO SAE 80W-90).

EVERY 1000 HOURS
Change hydraulic oil return filter. Change swing drive oil (7.0 L, CAT HYDO Advanced 10). Replace all fuel system filters simultaneously. Inspect engine mounts for cracking or compression — replace if deteriorated. Full inspection of all hydraulic control valves. Check and adjust engine valve clearances (inlet 0.38 mm, exhaust 0.64 mm). Replace serpentine belt. Inspect DEF/AdBlue dosing unit and injector.

EVERY 2000 HOURS
Complete hydraulic oil change — flush tank, change all filters, refill with 215 L of CAT HYDO Advanced 10. Replace final drive seals if any leakage observed. Full engine tune-up — fuel injector calibration, turbocharger inspection. Replace cooling system coolant (CAT ELC Extended Life Coolant). Inspect and measure cylinder bores.

EVERY 3000 HOURS
Replace final drive gear oil on both sides. Full hydraulic system pressure test and valve calibration. Inspect swing ring gear and pinion for wear. Major structural inspection — boom, stick, frame, including magnetic particle testing of welds.

FLUID SPECIFICATIONS
Engine Oil: CAT DEO 15W-40 or equivalent API CJ-4 rated
Hydraulic Oil: CAT HYDO Advanced 10 (ISO VG 46) — do not mix with other oils
Coolant: CAT ELC Extended Life Coolant, pre-mixed 50/50 — do not use tap water
Final Drive and Swing Drive: CAT FDAO SAE 80W-90
Grease: CAT Multipurpose Grease NLGI Grade 2 for all pins and swing bearing
DEF/AdBlue: 32.5% urea aqueous solution to ISO 22241 — do not use non-compliant fluid
Fuel: Ultra-low sulfur diesel (ULSD) — maximum 15 ppm sulfur content

WARNING SIGNS REQUIRING IMMEDIATE STOP
Engine oil pressure warning light on — stop engine within 10 seconds.
Coolant temperature above 105 degrees Celsius — stop, allow 10 minute cool-down.
Hydraulic oil temperature alarm — stop work, idle 5 minutes, check oil level and cooler.
Unusual knocking or rattling from engine — stop and call technician.
Hydraulic noise such as whining, chattering, or banging — stop and inspect.
Any hydraulic line spraying fluid — stop immediately, do not touch spray.
Visible coolant steam from engine compartment — stop and investigate.
Track shoe missing or broken track link — stop immediately.
Any structural crack in boom, stick, or frame — stop and tag out machine.
Black smoke with loss of power — check air filter and call technician.
Blue or white smoke from exhaust — engine mechanical issue, stop and inspect.
DEF warning light — fill DEF tank, machine will derate if ignored.

MAINTENANCE RECORD KEEPING
Record every service event: date, machine hours, service type, technician name, fluids used, parts replaced. Maintain CAT PM service records for warranty compliance. Use Cat App or Cat ET diagnostic tool for electronic fault code logging. Download and review fault code history every 250 hours.
