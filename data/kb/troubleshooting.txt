TROUBLESHOOTING COMMON PROBLEMS:

PROBLEM: Engine will not start.
CAUSE: Dead battery, fuel shutoff valve closed, fuel tank empty, clogged fuel filter.
SOLUTION: Check battery charge. Open fuel shutoff valve. Fill fuel tank. Replace fuel filter. Check glow plugs in cold weather.

PROBLEM: Engine overheating.
CAUSE: Low coolant level, clogged radiator, broken fan belt, faulty thermostat.
SOLUTION: Stop machine. Allow to cool. Check coolant level. Clean radiator fins. Inspect and replace fan belt. Test thermostat.

PROBLEM: Hydraulics slow or weak.
CAUSE: Low hydraulic oil, clogged hydraulic filter, worn pump, incorrect relief valve pressure.
SOLUTION: Check hydraulic oil level. Replace hydraulic filter. Test pump pressure. Adjust relief valve to 34,300 kPa specification.

PROBLEM: Excessive black smoke from exhaust.
CAUSE: Clogged air filter, over-fueling, engine overload.
SOLUTION: Clean or replace air filter. Check fuel injection pressure. Reduce load.

PROBLEM: Machine drifts or pulls to one side.
CAUSE: Track tension unequal, worn sprocket, pressure difference between travel motors.
SOLUTION: Check and equalize track tension. Inspect sprockets. Test travel motor pressure.

PROBLEM: Boom drops when engine is off.
CAUSE: Leaking cylinder seals, faulty boom lock valve.
SOLUTION: Inspect boom cylinder seals. Replace if leaking. Test boom lock valve function.

PROBLEM: Hydraulic oil temperature warning.
CAUSE: Low oil level, contaminated oil, clogged oil cooler.
SOLUTION: Check and top up oil. Change oil if contaminated. Clean oil cooler.

PROBLEM: Unusual noise from swing gear.
CAUSE: Low swing gear oil, worn swing bearing, debris in swing gear.
SOLUTION: Check swing gear oil. Inspect bearing play. Replace bearing if play exceeds 1.5 mm.

PROBLEM: Track keeps coming off.
CAUSE: Incorrect track tension, worn sprocket, bent track shoe.
SOLUTION: Adjust track tension — correct sag is 10-15 mm. Replace worn sprocket or idler.

PROBLEM: Seatbelt warning will not clear.
CAUSE: Seatbelt not fully latched, faulty seatbelt sensor.
SOLUTION: Re-latch seatbelt firmly. If warning persists, test seatbelt sensor connector. Replace sensor if faulty.

OPERATOR HEALTH AND SAFETY GUIDELINES:

FATIGUE MANAGEMENT:
Operator fatigue is a leading cause of construction site accidents.
Maximum recommended continuous operating time is 4 hours before a 30-minute break.
Night shift operators must have at least 8 hours of sleep before their shift.
Signs of fatigue: microsleeps, difficulty focusing, slow reaction times, irritability.
If you feel drowsy, stop the machine safely and notify your supervisor immediately.
Drink at least 500 ml of water per hour in hot conditions.

ERGONOMICS AND POSTURE:
Adjust the seat before starting so you can reach all controls comfortably.
Armrests should support your forearms while operating joysticks.
Take a 5-minute walk every 2 hours to prevent lower back strain.
Wrist and hand stretches are recommended before each shift.

HEAT STRESS PREVENTION:
At temperatures above 35 degrees Celsius, take 15-minute shade breaks every hour.
Heat exhaustion symptoms: heavy sweating, weakness, fast pulse, nausea.
Heat stroke: no sweating, hot dry skin, confusion — medical emergency, call for help immediately.
Keep water and electrolyte drinks in the cab.

NOISE AND VIBRATION:
Wear hearing protection when operating for more than 2 hours at high RPM.
Cab noise at normal operation is approximately 73 dB(A) with door closed.
Whole-body vibration limit: 8-hour exposure should not exceed 1.15 m/s2.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES:
Machine fire: Turn off engine. Use fire extinguisher. Exit cab. Move 50 meters upwind.
Machine tipping: Stay in cab and grip the handlebar. Do not jump — ROPS protects you.
Worker struck by machine: Stop immediately. Sound horn. Call emergency services.
Hydraulic line burst: Shut down hydraulics. Never touch spraying hydraulic fluid — it penetrates skin.

PRE-SHIFT CHECKLIST:
1. Walk around inspection for leaks, damage, and loose components.
2. Check engine oil, coolant, hydraulic oil, and fuel levels.
3. Inspect tracks for tension, wear, and missing pins.
4. Test horn, lights, reverse alarm, and windshield wipers.
5. Fasten seatbelt before starting engine.
6. Check all mirrors and cameras.
7. Test boom, stick, bucket, and swing functions slowly.
8. Log any defects before starting work.

PROXIMITY AND SITE SAFETY:
Exclusion zone: no workers within 5 meters of machine during operation.
Before swinging, sound horn and check mirrors.
On blind spots, use a spotter and hand signals.
Never lift loads over people.
When parking, lower all attachments to ground, engage park brake, remove key.
